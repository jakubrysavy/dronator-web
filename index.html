About me

“My name is Jakub. I have been a DJI drone pilot since 2024. I hold a remote pilot competency certificate for the A2 subcategory of the Open category, issued by the Civil Aviation Authority of the Czech Republic. Drones fascinated me before that, and since 2024 I have felt like a full-fledged member of the drone community. I fly in accordance with applicable laws, with the consent of the people concerned and, when needed, with permission from landowners. I edit my videos and music using freely available tools.”
